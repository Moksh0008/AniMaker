Content Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AniMaker is a creative platform for animators, writers, and artists. These guidelines help keep our community safe and respectful.

1. What You Can Create

  • Original artwork, illustrations, and character designs
  • Animations and short films
  • Stories, scripts, and written content
  • Fan art and transformative works (with proper attribution)
  • Collaborative projects with other creators

2. Content Guidelines

All content on AniMaker must be:

  • Appropriate for a general audience
  • Respectful of others and their work
  • Free from harassment, hate speech, or discrimination
  • Original or properly attributed

3. Prohibited Content

The following content is not allowed on AniMaker:

  • Explicit, violent, or graphic content
  • Content that promotes illegal activities
  • Content that infringes on copyright or intellectual property
  • Spam, scams, or misleading content
  • Content that targets or harasses individuals

4. Intellectual Property

Respect the creative work of others. Do not upload content that you do not own or have permission to share. Plagiarism and unauthorized use of others' work will result in content removal.

5. Reporting Content

If you see content that violates these guidelines, please report it through our contact page. We review all reports promptly.

6. Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition.

7. Contact

Questions about content policy? Reach out to us.
