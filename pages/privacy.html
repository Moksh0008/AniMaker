Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how AniMaker collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use AniMaker, we may collect:

  • Account information: Username, email address, display name, and profile picture
  • Content you create: Stories, images, videos, and other creative works
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and interaction patterns
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, and device identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve AniMaker's features
  • To personalize your experience
  • To communicate with you about your account and platform updates
  • To ensure platform safety and prevent abuse

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, an industry-standard backend platform. We implement encryption and access controls to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the rights and safety of AniMaker and its users

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your content

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session. For details, see our Cookie Policy.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us.
